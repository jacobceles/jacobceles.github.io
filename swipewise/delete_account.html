Delete your SwipeWise account and data

Last updated: 2026-08-12.

SwipeWise does not require an account. If you never signed in, there is nothing here to delete — everything the app knows lives on your phone, and uninstalling removes it.

If you did sign in, this page explains how to delete that account and everything stored with it.

From inside the app

  1. Open SwipeWise.
  2. Go to Profile.
  3. Tap Delete my account and confirm.

This is immediate and cannot be undone.

If you no longer have the app

Email jacobceles@gmail.com from the address you signed in with, asking for your SwipeWise account to be deleted. Requests are actioned within 30 days. We ask you to write from the signed-in address because it is the only way to tell that the account is yours — we hold no other identifying information about you.

What is deleted

  • Your account — the Google sign-in record itself.
  • Your wallet backup, if you turned backup on: the cards you added, your edits to them (nicknames, credit limits, due dates), which catalog product each card maps to, your preferences and your muted stores.
  • Your bank connection, if you linked one: the customer record held with our bank-data provider, and every bank connected under it.
  • Everything on your phone — the local database, including your transaction history.

Deletion is immediate and permanent. There is no recovery window and no archived copy, so a deleted wallet cannot be restored onto a new phone afterwards.

What is not deleted, and why

  • Crash reports. These are pseudonymous — keyed to a Firebase installation id that you never see and that we cannot map back to a person. We could not find your reports in order to delete them even if you asked. They expire on Firebase's own retention schedule, typically within 90 days.

Nothing else is retained. Your transactions were never uploaded in the first place, so there is no server-side copy of them to delete.

Deleting without deleting your account

If you only want to stop backing up, turn Back Up My Wallet off in Profile and delete the stored backup from the same screen. Your account remains, and the app keeps working.

Signing out is not deletion: it leaves your data on the phone and your backup on our servers, so that changing phones does not cost you your wallet.
