Delete your SwipeWise account and data

Last updated: 2026-08-12.

SwipeWise does not require an account. If you never signed in there is nothing here to delete — everything the app knows is on your phone, and uninstalling removes it.

In the app

  1. Open SwipeWise and go to Profile.
  2. Tap Delete my account and confirm.

If you no longer have the app

Email jacobceles@gmail.com from the address you signed in with, asking for your account to be deleted. Requests are completed within 30 days. We ask you to write from that address because it is the only way to tell the account is yours — we hold nothing else that identifies you.

What is deleted

  • Your account — the Google sign-in record itself.
  • Your wallet backup, if you turned backup on: your cards, your edits to them, your preferences and your muted stores.
  • Your bank connection, if you had one, including the customer record held with our bank-data provider.
  • Everything on your phone, including your transaction history.

This is immediate and permanent. There is no recovery window and no archived copy, so a deleted wallet cannot be restored onto a new phone afterwards.

What is kept

Only crash reports. They are tied to an anonymous installation id that we cannot trace back to a person, so we could not find yours in order to delete them. They expire on Google's own schedule, typically within 90 days. Your transactions were never uploaded, so there is no copy of them to delete.

Stopping backup without deleting

Turn Back Up My Wallet off in Profile. Nothing further is uploaded, and the copy already stored is kept so you can still restore it on a new phone. Deleting your account is what erases that copy.

Signing out is not deletion: your data stays on your phone and your backup stays with us, so that changing phones does not cost you your wallet.
